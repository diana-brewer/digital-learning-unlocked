The Sore Throat Case, Hand-Built: A Branching Scenario Demo

A 19-year-old with a sore throat he cannot swallow around. Make decisions. See consequences. Learn from the path.
Demonstration note: This is a prototype built for the 2026 APP Fellowship Conference, adapted from the published MedEdPORTAL case in the Workshop Guide's Running Example (the same 19-year-old you will meet twice more today: as an AI-generated case in Section 3, and grounded in the published case files in Section 4). In a real implementation, this would run in H5P (free, open-source) embedded in your LMS. No programming required, H5P uses a visual editor. What this demo does well: it captures clinical reasoning, every decision has consequences, the numbers are identical for every learner, and the reasoning path IS the assessment. What it cannot do: mimic reality. Real patients are not multiple choice, and this case only responds to what its author pre-imagined.

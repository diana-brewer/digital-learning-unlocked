Rebuild steps (original manual path):

  1. Go to notebooklm.google.com (Google renamed it Gemini Notebook — same place). Sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click Create new notebook. Name it Pericarditis Teaching Case.
  3. Click Add source → Upload and add the three PDFs above (drag-and-drop works). Wait ~30 seconds for processing (the sources show brief summaries when ready).
  4. Sanity-check: ask the notebook What are the diagnostic criteria for acute pericarditis? — you should get an answer with numbered citations pointing to your documents.
  5. Open the Studio panel → Audio Overview. Choose format Deep Dive (or The Brief — under 2 minutes, single narrator) and click generate. It takes a few minutes — generate it the day before and leave the notebook open on your laptop for the live demo. In the session you press play for ~15 seconds, no waiting.
  6. Optional but recommended: also generate a Study Guide and FAQ from the Studio panel before the session — they make nice things to show if the Audio Overview is being finicky.
  7. Test the room WiFi that morning; keep the notebook open in a browser tab. If WiFi fails at demo time, follow the backup script in the Presenter Notes (90-second recording or screenshots).
